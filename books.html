A Good Girls Guide to Murder:

The case is closed. Five years ago, schoolgirl Andie Bell was murdered
by Sal Singh. The police know he did it. Everyone in town knows he did it.

But having grown up in the same small town that was consumed by the crime,
Pippa Fitz-Amobi isn't so sure. When she chooses the case as the topic for her
final project, she starts to uncover secrets that someone in town desperately
wants to stay hidden. And if the real killer is still out there, how far will
they go to keep Pip from the truth...?

And Then There Were None:

And Then There Were None by Agatha Christie is a gripping mystery novel about
ten strangers who are invited to a secluded island off the coast of Devon by an
unknown host. Although they have nothing in common at first glance, each of them
hides a dark secret from their past. Soon, the guests find themselves trapped on
the island, where strange events begin to unfold and their situation becomes
increasingly frightening.

As the guests are mysteriously killed one by one, they realize that the murders
follow the pattern of a nursery rhyme displayed throughout the house. With no way
to escape and suspicion growing among them, everyone becomes a potential suspect.
Full of suspense, unexpected twists, and clever clues, this classic thriller keeps
readers guessing until the very end as they try to uncover the identity of the killer.

The Silent Patient :

The Silent Patient by Alex Michaelides is a gripping psychological thriller about
Alicia Berenson, a famous painter whose seemingly perfect life takes a dark turn
when she is accused of killing her husband, Gabriel. After the incident, Alicia
stops speaking completely and refuses to explain what happened. Her silence turns
the case into a mystery that captures public attention, leaving everyone wondering
what really happened that night.

Theo Faber, a criminal psychotherapist, becomes determined to uncover the truth
behind Alicia’s silence. As he begins working with her, he finds himself drawn
deeper into a complicated web of secrets, hidden motives, and unexpected discoveries.
Filled with suspense, psychological twists, and a shocking mystery, The Silent Patient
keeps readers guessing until the very end, challenging them to question how well
they really know the people around them.

Project Hail Mary

Project Hail Mary by Andy Weir is a science fiction adventure about an astronaut
who wakes up alone on a spaceship with no memory of who he is or how he got there.
As his memories slowly return, he realizes that he is on a mission to save Earth
from a mysterious threat. With limited resources and no one to help him at first,
he must rely on his scientific knowledge, creativity, and determination to find a
solution.

The story is a mix of science, mystery, humor, and unexpected discoveries that
keep readers curious about what will happen next. It explores the importance of
friendship, teamwork, and never giving up, even when the odds seem impossible. With
its clever storytelling and fascinating space adventure, Project Hail Mary is a great
read for anyone who enjoys science fiction with a touch of humor and emotion.

Divergent Saga

The Divergent Collection by Veronica Roth is an exciting dystopian series set
in a futuristic society divided into factions, each representing a different virtue.
The story follows Tris, a young girl who discovers that she doesn’t fit into the
rules of her society. As she struggles to find her identity, she faces dangerous
challenges, unexpected betrayals, and choices that could change her life forever.

The collection includes all three books of the trilogy — Divergent, Insurgent,
and Allegiant — along with A Divergent Collection, which explores the story from
Tobias’s perspective. Filled with action, suspense, romance, and shocking twists,
the series explores themes of courage, loyalty, sacrifice, and freedom. It takes
readers on an unforgettable journey through a world where one choice can transform
everything.

Dune

Set on the desert planet Arrakis, Dune is the story of the boy Paul Atreides,
heir to a noble family tasked with ruling an inhospitable world where the only
thing of value is the “spice” melange, a drug capable of extending life and enhancing
consciousness. Coveted across the known universe, melange is a prize worth killing
for...

When House Atreides is betrayed, the destruction of Paul’s family will set the
boy on a journey toward a destiny greater than he could ever have imagined. And as
he evolves into the mysterious man known as Muad’Dib, he will bring to fruition
humankind’s most ancient and unattainable dream.

Our cut of salt.

The House of Rust is a haunting story about family, memory, and the secrets of
the past. The story follows Marina, a young woman who decides to visit her grandmother
Nuhad’s childhood home in Haifa, a place her family lost during the Nakba in 1948.
The house has a dark reputation, surrounded by strange occurrences and mysterious
disappearances. Despite her mother Haifa’s warnings, Marina is determined to uncover
the truth about her family’s history and reconnect with a culture she has never
fully known.

However, as Marina explores the house and discovers more about its past, she
begins to experience disturbing changes, and the family’s buried secrets slowly
come to light. As three generations of women become connected to the house’s haunting
history, they must confront their painful past before it consumes them. Blending
supernatural horror with themes of identity, heritage, and generational trauma,
The House of Rust offers a chilling story that goes beyond a typical ghost story.

The house built on alligator bones.

The story follows Dartrine Beaumont, who believes she has no family left after
her mother’s sudden death. However, she soon discovers that she has wealthy relatives
living in Florida and travels to her grandmother’s mansion on Amelia Island. There,
she finds herself competing for an unusual inheritance: a family-owned alligator
farming business.

Things take a mysterious turn when strange incidents begin happening on the
farms, and an old family curse comes to light. As Dart uncovers hidden secrets,
family rivalries, and a dark history connected to a legendary alligator, she must
decide whether to accept her family’s troubled legacy or choose a different path
for herself. Blending mystery, suspense, and Southern Gothic horror, the story explores
the dark side of family ties and the secrets that can haunt generations.

Buyer Beware

If these walls could talk, they’d scream...

When Ellie moves to 1 Delaney Row, she hopes to find a fresh start—a place where
no one knows her name, her history, or her secrets. But what she doesn’t know is
that her new home is already hiding someone else’s secrets—and the people determined
to keep them are watching her.

As Ellie starts to unravel the house’s disturbing backstory, coming closer to
the shocking mystery at its center, she unwittingly puts herself on a deadly collision
course not just with her new home’s history, but with her own as well.

A puzzle box of a thriller full of mind-boggling twists and turns, Buyer Beware is
a chilling exploration of the dark secrets that any house can hold—and of the
lengths we’ll go to start over.
