  1. Mystery: A mystery thriller is a type of story that combines mystery, suspense, and danger. It often involves a crime, a secret, or a strange event that needs to be solved. The story gives readers clues while keeping them excited about what will happen next. Unexpected twists make the story more interesting.

  2. Science Fiction: Science fiction, or sci-fi, is a genre that explores how science and technology can affect our lives and society. It includes both real and imaginary scientific ideas. Common themes are space travel, robots, artificial intelligence, and time travel. These stories help readers imagine how future discoveries could change the world.

  3. Horror: A horror thriller is a genre that combines suspense and excitement with fear and scary elements. It often includes mysterious events, creepy settings, and unexpected twists. The story keeps readers curious and nervous about what will happen next. It creates a sense of fear while making readers eager to discover the truth.
